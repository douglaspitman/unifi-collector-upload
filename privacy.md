# Privacy Policy — UniFi Collector Upload

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

## Overview

UniFi Collector Upload is a personal application used to transfer home infrastructure monitoring reports from a privately managed computer to the owner's Google Drive account.

It is not a commercial service and is not available for public use.

## Information Processed

The application transfers monitoring reports that may include device names, network status, system performance measurements, equipment health information, and security event summaries.

Reports are generated locally and uploaded only to the Google Drive account authorized by the owner.

## Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth authorization and the Google Drive API to transfer files.

Although the authorization may provide broad Google Drive access, the application is intended to use that access only to maintain its designated monitoring reports.

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's computer and are not published in this website's repository.

## Data Sharing and Retention

Monitoring information is not sold or shared with advertisers. Uploaded reports are stored in the owner's Google Drive account, subject to Google's applicable policies.

The owner controls the stored reports and may delete them from Google Drive or revoke the application's access through Google Account settings.
